## Props

<PropsTable component="badge" />

The badge names no colour of its own. Every variant resolves against `colorPalette`, which is an
inherited style prop — so `<Badge colorPalette="green">` and a `colorPalette` on any ancestor pick
the palette, and the four variants decide how much of it the badge spends.
