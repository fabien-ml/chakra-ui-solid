# Copyright and trademark are separate controls

A licence to copy a file is not a licence to use a mark as an identity. Chakra's bolt (`BlitzIcon`, `BlitzFillIcon`, the `LogoIcon` glyph) and `favicon.ico` are MIT files and are copied with attribution (see the notice policy in [0045](0045-notice-per-package.md)). Trademark is carried separately by four safeguards:

| # | Safeguard |
|---|---|
| 1 | The logotype reads `chakra-ui-solid`, never `chakra` alone; site chrome (titles, nav) never says "Chakra UI". Body copy may, where it is the subject (home, migration page) |
| 2 | None of Chakra's wordmark lettering is reproduced: the glyph travels, the set type does not |
| 3 | No social card and no combined mark: no asset pairs their mark with ours as one lockup |
| 4 | The disclaimer ("independent, unofficial, not affiliated with or endorsed by Chakra Systems Inc.") appears verbatim above the fold on the docs home and in every page footer, with chakra-ui.com as a live link |

Row 4 carries the weight: under a mark-derived name, beside the upstream's own glyph, a reader's first question is whether this is the official project, and the disclaimer is the only thing that answers it. The link turns the disclaimer into a redirect. The same disclaimer sits near the top of the root README.

The palette arriving through the preset is licensed code doing its job, not brand use. Framework logos on the site are nominative use of another project's mark, shown unmodified.

## Considered options

- **Ban every Chakra mark and redraw the icons.** Rejected: a redrawn bolt close enough to read as the same mark is a derivative of the mark, a worse trademark position than the honest copy plus disclaimer.
- **Ship no mark at all.** A real option, but it loses the one thing the glyph does: telling a Chakra reader within a second what this library targets. The disclaimer keeps that from becoming a claim.

## Reopen when

Wordmark lettering is reproduced, a combined mark or social card is built, or any use reads as the site's identity rather than a link to the project it ports.

## Consequences

The disclaimer is load-bearing, not courtesy. No Chakra visual identity beyond the copied glyph and favicon may be used.
